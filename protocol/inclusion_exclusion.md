# Inclusion and Exclusion Criteria

## Purpose

This document defines the eligibility criteria before full screening to improve consistency, transparency, and reproducibility.

---

# Inclusion Criteria

A study is eligible for inclusion when it satisfies the relevant criterias below.

## IC1 — Agricultural Domain

The study must concern an agricultural or farming context, including at least one of:

- crop production;
- irrigation;
- smart farming;
- precision agriculture;
- livestock;
- pasture management;
- farm-resource management;
- agricultural environmental management.

---

## IC2 — Multi-Agent Component

The study must explicitly involve:

- a Multi-Agent System (MAS);
- multiple autonomous or interacting agents; or
- a clearly defined agent-based decision architecture involving meaningful interaction between agents.

---

## IC3 — Decision or Coordination Relevance

The study must address at least one relevant MAS function, such as:

- agricultural decision-making;
- communication, cooperation, coordination;
- negotiation;
- resource management and allocation;
- conflict resolution;
- planning;
- distributed control.

---

## IC4 — Relevant Agricultural or Environmental Outcome

The study should investigate or report at least one relevant agricultural or environmental aspect, including:

- irrigation;
- water allocation;
- crop management;
- livestock management;
- resource efficiency;
- energy management;
- environmental sustainability;
- greenhouse-gas emissions;
- soil or nutrient management;
- pasture or land management.

---

## IC5 — Publication Type

The study should be a:

- peer-reviewed journal/article; or
- peer-reviewed conference paper.

---

## IC6 — Language

The full study must be available in English.

---

## IC7 — Publication Period

The primary review window is:

**2010–2026**

Earlier studies may be considered when identified through backward citation searching and when they represent important foundational work directly relevant to the review.

---

## IC8 — Sufficient Information

The study must provide enough information to determine:

- the role of the agents;
- the application domain;
- the type of interaction or decision-making;
- the methodology or system architecture.

---

# Exclusion Criteria

## EC1 — No Agricultural Context

Exclude studies that do not concern agriculture, farming, irrigation, livestock, or related agricultural-resource management.

---

## EC2 — No Meaningful Multi-Agent Interaction

Exclude systems that use the word "agent" but do not actually contain meaningful interaction between autonomous or decision-making agents.

---

## EC3 — Pure IoT Monitoring

Exclude studies that only:

- collect sensor data;
- monitor farm conditions;
- transmit IoT measurements;

without meaningful multi-agent decision-making, communication, coordination, or resource allocation.

---

## EC4 — Conventional Machine Learning Only

Exclude studies that only use:

- classification;
- regression;
- deep learning;
- computer vision;
- forecasting;

without a meaningful interaction between multiple agents.

---

## EC5 — Insufficient Methodological Detail

Exclude studies where there is insufficient information to determine:

- what the agents are;
- how agents interact;
- what decisions they make;
- how the MAS operates.

---

## EC6 — Non-Peer-Reviewed Literature

Normally exclude:

- blog posts;
- commercial webpages;
- news articles;
- presentations;
- posters without sufficient methodological detail;
- non-academic opinion pieces.

Potentially relevant grey literature may be recorded separately but will not form part of the primary evidence synthesis unless the protocol is explicitly amended.

---

# Special Case: Agent-Based Simulation

Agent-based simulations may be included when agents explicitly:

- make decisions;
- interact;
- communicate, coordinate, negotiate; or
- compete/cooperate over agricultural resources.

A simulation should not be excluded merely because it has not been deployed on a physical farm.

---

# Borderline Studies

If eligibility is uncertain during title/abstract screening: **Mark it for full-text assessment.**

Study should normally progress to full-text screening when there is insufficient information in the title or abstract to confidently apply an exclusion criterion.

## Final Study Selection text for the report

Five bibliographic databases yielded 186 records. After manual resolution of duplicate candidates in Rayyan, 84 duplicate records were removed, leaving 102 unique records for title/abstract screening. Fifty-one records were excluded and 51 reports were sought for full-text retrieval. Sixteen reports could not be retrieved, leaving 35 reports for eligibility assessment. One report was excluded because its evaluated application concerned landscape/garden irrigation rather than agricultural production or farm management, and 34 reports were initially included.

One-generation backward and forward citation searching from these 34 initially included reports identified 24 additional records. Three records were excluded before full-text retrieval. Of 21 reports sought, one could not be retrieved and 20 were assessed for eligibility. One report was excluded because it did not substantively implement meaningful multi-agent interaction, leaving 19 additional reports.

The final review therefore included **53 reports/publications**, reconciled into **44 study families** for evidence-independence sensitivity analysis.

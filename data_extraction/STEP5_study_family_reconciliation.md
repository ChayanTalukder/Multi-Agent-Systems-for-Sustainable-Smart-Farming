## Reconciliation rule

A shared software platform, topic, or research tradition **does not automatically create one study family**.

Publications were grouped only when they substantially reuse the same underlying empirical case, dataset, model experiment, or represent a clear publication extension of the same study. This prevents both double-counting and excessive collapsing of genuinely independent applications.
